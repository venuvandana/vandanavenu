// 05. case studies

Real problems I've solved.

The kinds of issues I handle in my current role. Client names are kept private. Click a case to open it.

case #01 · identity

Users locked out after an SSO change

SAML 2.0IdP configCertificates
+
problem

A client's employees suddenly could not log in to the learning platform through their company SSO.

investigation

Captured the SAML response, compared the certificate and attribute mapping on both sides, and checked the login logs.

fix

Found a mismatch after a change on the identity provider side and guided the client's IT team to correct it.

result

Login restored for all users, plus a short checklist the client can use for future SSO changes.

case #02 · email delivery

Platform emails not reaching inboxes

SendGridSPF / DKIMDNS
+
problem

Learners weren't receiving invitations and reminders, so course completion was dropping.

investigation

Traced individual messages in SendGrid activity logs to see if they were delivered, bounced or blocked.

fix

Identified the cause (domain authentication or recipient-side filtering) and shared the exact steps with the client's IT team.

result

Emails delivering again, and a clear explanation the client could pass on internally.

case #03 · automation

Automation rule that silently didn't fire

Event rulesUser dataDebugging
+
problem

New users were supposed to be enrolled in onboarding content automatically, but some never got it.

investigation

Traced one affected user's event history step by step against the rule's conditions.

fix

Found a condition that didn't match how those users' data was set up, and corrected the rule.

result

Every new user now lands in the right content, with no manual workaround needed.
